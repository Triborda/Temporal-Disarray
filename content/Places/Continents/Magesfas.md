**Magesfas** is the main land of [[Nianagand]] consisting of seven distinct [[regions]]
___
The [[regions]] of Magesfas are:
- [[Tismarch]]
- [[Sutesar]]
- [[Mondrai]]
- [[Molanth]]
- [[Heandal]]
- [[Litsbow]]
- [[Evinace]]
---
Most of the population of [[Nianagand]] live on the mainland of **Magesfas**
