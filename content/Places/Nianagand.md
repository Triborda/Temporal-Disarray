**Nianagand** is the world we live on, another name for the [[Material Plane]]. 
___
Nianagand consists of the main land of [[Magesfas]] with it's seven regions, the island regions of [[Wildemount]] and [[Slathilia]] and some smaller, mostly uninhabited islands.
