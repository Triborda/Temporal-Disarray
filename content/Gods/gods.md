The **gods** are the beings that control the laws of nature that we know. when a single god dies disaster will surely follow
___
There are many gods of many laws of nature, but only one god resides over a given law of nature, though they may have many names.

There are gods who only rule over the [[Material Plane]], some who only rule over the [[feywild]] and some who only rule over the [[shadowfell]]
