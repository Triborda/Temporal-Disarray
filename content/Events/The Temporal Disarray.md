**The Temporal Disarray** is the event that sees all of [[Nianagand]] splintered through time. The Temporal Disarray began when the [[gods|god]] of time [[Chronos]] was killed.

[[The Adventurers]] seek to restore time to its normal progression to stop [[Nianagand]] from being torn apart
